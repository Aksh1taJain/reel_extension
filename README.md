# reel_extension
Lets say you are preparing for an exam but you suddenly want to scroll insta. You can't waste your precious time right? But scrolling insta is also important to help you function better! <br> <br>
So, to escape this dilemma, I introduce you this reel_extension <br>
- Its an extension to help you watch instagram reels on a speed greater than 2x. It is made to help everyone save their time while doomscrolling and consume more content in lesser time.<br><br>
But before you start doomscrolling you have to set timer and here's the catch you can't scroll for more than 10 minutes and then the extension locks web instagram for 50 minutes.
<br>
Now you must be thinking 10 minutes is very less.
but if every reel you  are watching is 1 min long in those 10 minutes and you have set the speed at 3x you can watch 30 reels instead of 10, and 30 reels 1 min lon is equivalent to wasting 30 minutes doom scrolling.
<br>
You've just saved 20 minutes!
<br>
- It also automatically scrolls your reels too, if u want to pause just tap it you don't even have to waste your time swiping the screen!
